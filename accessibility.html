Accessibility

Access is operational.

City919 is committed to making its public information and service-support channels usable by people with disabilities.

Our standard

We work toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA for public web content, including keyboard access, clear focus indicators, readable contrast, meaningful labels, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.

Known limitations

Third-party email, telephony, document, or scheduling experiences may have accessibility characteristics outside City919’s direct control. We will provide an alternative format or contact path when requested.

Request help

If you encounter a barrier, tell us the page, task, assistive technology, and preferred response format. Email contact@emt919.com. We aim to acknowledge accessibility requests within two business days.

Continuous review

Accessibility is reviewed as content and product workflows change. This statement does not claim third-party certification or conformance testing that has not been completed.

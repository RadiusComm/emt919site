Buyer checklist

Put every assumption in writing.

A practical review sequence for command, operations, IT, security, legal, records, accessibility, and purchasing stakeholders.

Product and policy

  • Approved users, call types, languages, and locations
  • AI-first versus live-interpreter-only configuration
  • Mandatory live-escalation triggers
  • Disconnection, low-quality audio, and unavailable-service procedure
  • Training, acceptance testing, and operational owner

Security and privacy

  • Architecture and named subprocessor review
  • Certification boundaries accurately represented
  • Authentication, authorization, logging, and agency isolation
  • Audio, transcript, and audit-log retention
  • Incident contacts, notification terms, and evidence access
  • Accessibility review and accommodation path

Commercial and legal

  • Licensed-user definition and quantity
  • Minimums, optional services, and live-interpreter costs
  • Implementation, training, support, and renewal scope
  • Contract term, termination, data return, and deletion
  • Insurance, W-9, corporate documents, and purchasing authority
  • Grant or cooperative-purchasing statements independently verified

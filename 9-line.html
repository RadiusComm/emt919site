9-LINE

Call out. Get called back.
Keep the conversation going.

9-Line brings outbound calls, incoming calls, and SMS/MMS messaging to EMT919 officers. A communication tool for the follow-up, the callback, and the details that come next.

THREE WAYS TO STAY IN CONTACT

Voice when it matters.
A message when it helps.

01 / OUTBOUND

Make the next call.

Reach a resident, return a call, or follow up on an earlier conversation using 9-Line.

Example: arrange a time for a witness to speak with an officer.

02 / INCOMING

Keep a path back.

Receive incoming calls so a contact can call back and continue the conversation.

Example: take a callback about a previously discussed community concern.

03 / SMS & MMS

Put details in a message.

Exchange text and multimedia messages for agency-approved communication. SMS supports text; MMS supports media such as photos.

Example: send a meeting location or receive a non-sensitive location photo when policy permits.

Examples illustrate possible workflows, not documented customer incidents. Call and message delivery depend on connectivity, recipient devices, carrier support, and service configuration.

BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Questions worth asking.

Is 9-Line the same as dialing #919?

No. 9-Line is the name of the officer calling and messaging product. Do not assume that dialing #919 launches 9-Line or that the two services have identical access requirements.

Does every officer get a separate number?

Number assignment and caller-ID behavior are part of the agency setup review. Ask the EMT919 team to confirm the available configuration before adopting a workflow.

Are calls recorded and messages retained?

Recording, storage, retention, export, and access terms must be confirmed specifically for 9-Line in the agency's agreement. This page does not promise automatic recording or a particular retention period.

How does an agency get started?

Request a briefing with your approximate officer count and intended calling and messaging uses. Availability, onboarding, service configuration, and commercial terms are confirmed with the EMT919 team. Do not send case details or protected information.

Not a replacement for 911, dispatch, or radio.

Use your agency's designated emergency and dispatch channels for urgent response. Do not assume a call or message has been received, read, or monitored.

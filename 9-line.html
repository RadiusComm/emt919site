Case Lines & Secure Call Recording · 9/Line Team

Give an officer a primary work line plus additional numbers labeled for separate investigations. Select the line before calling or messaging, see which line an incoming call reaches, and keep each line’s communications organized with its case reference. Investigation labels stay internal—not in caller ID or recipient-facing messages.

Agency-approved inbound and outbound recording connects protected audio and transcripts to the call record. Visible recording status, authenticated playback, permission-checked downloads and generic post-call email links support controlled access. Recording, supervisor listening and officer location each require separate permissions.

Administrators control number assignments, recording policies, retention and access history. Number allowances, additional charges, required notices and feature activation are confirmed before purchase. SMS consent and opt-outs remain enforced across line changes.

Live Officer Location · 9/Line Team

See the officer’s device-reported position alongside a monitored call, with a map pin, accuracy radius and last-updated time. Call-start location and updates during the call add context to the call information—not the civilian’s location.

Call-scoped sharing requires an enabled native app build, officer permission and a visible sharing indicator. Sharing stops when the call ends. Location viewing and call listening have separate agency-scoped permissions and audit controls; no off-duty tracking.

Approximate, stale or unavailable positions are clearly labeled. Locked-screen updates depend on supported devices, granted permissions and system conditions. Native-device support and feature activation are confirmed before purchase.

More agency controls

Assignment history, consent evidence, attachment status, email-delivery tracking, budget warnings and audit logs. Administration does not automatically grant permission to listen, read messages or export records.

Calling, devices and availability

Confirm caller ID, routing and supported devices. Connectivity is required; background or locked-phone receiving must be verified for each device. 9/Line is not the same as dialing #919.

Transcript access and retention

A forwarded email grants no access. Officers sign in with current record permissions. Audio processing, retained recordings, holds, deletion and exports follow separate agency-approved rules. Carrier calls and SMS/MMS are not end-to-end encrypted.

Supervision and language access

Approve eligible calls, supervisor scope, officer visibility and required notices. Live EMT interpretation and human escalation are 9/Line Team options; supported languages and integrations require validation. Post-call transcription is not live translation.

9-LINE SMS/MMS

Consent first.
Clear choices after.

Civilians receive 9-Line text or picture messages only after consent or when they initiate a message to an officer's dedicated work number.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. 9-Line is not an emergency service; call 911 for emergencies.

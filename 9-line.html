02 / CALL RECORDS

A protected record after the call.

Turn an eligible completed call into a timestamped transcript, with a generic email notification linking the officer to a protected viewer.

  • Encrypted transcript storage and authorized encrypted downloads.
  • No transcript attachment or conversation details in notification emails.
  • Processing status, access history and preserved corrections.

For agency-approved calls only. Machine-generated text requires review; processing, audio retention and required notices follow agency policy.

03 / SUPERVISION

Listen. Support. Stay accountable.

Give authorized supervisors a listen-only connection to both sides of an eligible live call, within their approved agency and team scope.

  • Listen-only audio: no whisper or barge-in.
  • Officer-visible monitoring indicator and logged joins and departures.
  • Leave monitoring without ending the officer's call.

Part of the Officer package scope. Access requires agency authorization and applicable notices; calls are not automatically monitored.

BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Questions worth asking.

Is 9-Line the same as dialing #919?

No. 9-Line is the name of the officer calling and messaging product. Do not assume that dialing #919 launches 9-Line or that the two services have identical access requirements.

Which package is right for our agency?

Choose the Officer package scope for everyday calling, messaging, protected transcripts, listen-only supervision and agency management. Advanced Officer expands that scope with team workflows, identity, records and language-access options. Some described capabilities are in development; we confirm availability and activation requirements in your agency proposal before purchase.

Does every officer get a separate number?

Enrolled officers can select a dedicated work number through the agency's approved setup. Use that number for work contacts to keep the officer's personal phone number private. Messaging capability and number assignments depend on the agency's service configuration.

Can residents call or text the officer back?

Residents can call an officer's dedicated 9-Line number. The Officer package also covers two-way texts and photo messages when messaging is configured and enabled. The service is a follow-up contact channel, not a continuously monitored emergency line.

How does transcript email delivery work?

The transcript service is designed to process eligible completed calls under agency policy. When a transcript is ready, a generic notification goes to the officer's verified agency email. Its link opens a sign-in-protected viewer with authorized encrypted downloads; forwarding the email does not grant access. No-answer or policy-excluded calls do not produce a transcript. Confirm service activation in your proposal.

Is supervisor monitoring part of the Officer package?

Yes—listen-only supervision is part of the Officer package scope, not reserved for Advanced Officer. When enabled, authorized supervisors choose eligible calls, with officer visibility, required notices and an audit trail. Whisper, barge-in and automatic monitoring of every call are not included.

Where does EMT919 interpretation fit?

Live EMT919 language access and human-interpreter escalation are Advanced Officer integration options, subject to availability and supported-language validation. Transcribing a call after it ends is not live interpretation. Explore EMT919 interpretation.

Are calls recorded and messages retained?

Audio processing for transcription, retained recordings, messaging records and exports have separate policy and access requirements. Their availability, retention, holds and deletion terms must be confirmed in the agency agreement. This page does not promise automatic recording or a fixed retention period. Carrier calls and SMS/MMS should not be described as end-to-end encrypted.

How does an agency get started?

Request a briefing with your approximate officer count and intended calling and messaging uses. Availability, onboarding, service configuration, and commercial terms are confirmed with the EMT919 team. Do not send case details or protected information.

Not a replacement for 911, dispatch, or radio.

Use your agency's designated emergency and dispatch channels for urgent response. Do not assume a call or message has been received, read, or monitored.

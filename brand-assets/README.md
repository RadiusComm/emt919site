# EMT919 approved branding kit

These files are the approved EMT919 logo family, updated July 2026. The primary wordmark treatment uses burgundy `#9B1D32` for **EMT**, the approved full-color responder shield, green **919**, and the navy tagline. Preserve the 3:1 aspect ratio, clear space, typography, shield artwork, and color relationships.

## Core digital colors

- EMT burgundy: `#9B1D32`
- Header navy: `#08111F`
- Tagline on light backgrounds: approved navy artwork from the master
- Tagline on dark backgrounds: `#FFFFFF`
- Shield and 919: preserve the supplied full-color artwork; do not recolor or flatten it

## Usage rules

- Use the light-background family on white or very light neutral surfaces.
- Use the burgundy/white-tagline header lockup on the EMT919 navy header.
- Use the alternate white treatment only when burgundy does not provide sufficient contrast.
- Do not rebuild the wordmark with live text, separate the shield from the wordmark, crop the tagline, stretch the artwork, add effects, or substitute unapproved colors.

The earlier fire-engine-red header experiment is superseded by the burgundy treatment and is not part of the current approved kit.

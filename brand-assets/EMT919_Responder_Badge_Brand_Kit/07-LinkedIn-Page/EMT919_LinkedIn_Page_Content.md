## About section — recommended

EMT919—Emergency Multitranslator—helps authorized public-safety responders begin multilingual conversations from the phones they already carry.

An agency can configure a dedicated line or #919 access for turn-by-turn communication in the field. EMT919 supports access to more than 300 AI and EMT Live language options, depending on the agency’s configuration and contracted service. Responders can begin the exchange, keep roles clear, and move to a live interpreter when policy, language, or the situation requires it.

EMT919 is designed for law enforcement, fire and EMS, corrections, campus and school public safety, animal control, and other government response teams. The objective is practical: reduce confusion, support de-escalation, gather essential initial information, explain next steps, and help responders maintain control of the scene during the first critical moments.

Security, privacy, retention, language availability, live-interpreter access, and escalation workflows are governed by agency configuration and agreement. Visit our Trust Center for current details or request an agency briefing at emt.city919.com.

EMT919 is a service of Fast Dial, Inc. d/b/a City919.

## Pinned “How it works” post

**Dial. Talk. Continue.**

1. **Dial** — Call the dedicated agency line or use configured #919 access from an authorized phone.
2. **Talk** — Begin a turn-by-turn multilingual exchange with clear speaker roles.
3. **Continue** — Keep working the call and move to EMT Live when agency policy, language, or the situation requires it.

No public app download. No separate field device. The service and available options depend on the agency’s configuration and agreement.

## Procurement post

## Company-page voice guide

- Write like a shift briefing: short sentences, direct verbs, and operational language.
- Lead with the outcome: communication, scene control, de-escalation, responder safety, and accountability.
- Use “helps,” “supports,” and “is designed to” for operational outcomes unless a stronger claim has documented evidence.
- Qualify availability with “depending on agency configuration and agreement.”
- Call the live-interpreter service **EMT Live** in public copy.
- Use **Emergency Multitranslator** as the official expanded name.
- Do not name agencies, pilots, customers, certifications, response times, grant programs, or performance statistics without written verification and permission.
- Do not describe “CJIS-aligned” as a certification or badge.
- Do not promise universal automatic escalation or guaranteed 24/7 live-interpreter availability unless the applicable contract expressly supports the claim.

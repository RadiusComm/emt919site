# EMT919 Responder Badge Brand Kit

Version 1.4 - July 2026

This is the approved EMT919 identity package for new public-facing work.

Official expanded name: **Emergency Multi-Translator**.

## Which logo to use

### Dark backgrounds

Use `01-Primary-Logo/EMT919_Primary_Dark_Transparent_1200x400.png` for most websites, presentations, video, and digital layouts on navy, black, charcoal, or dark photography.

Use the 600-pixel file for compact web placement, the 1800-pixel file for high-density displays, and the 3600-pixel master for large layouts.

### White or light backgrounds

Use `02-Light-Background/EMT919_Light_Transparent_1200x400.png`. This rebuilt version uses a restrained forest green for 919, true open counters in both 9s, an open white badge interior, and black for the `Emergency Multi-Translator` descriptor. It is optimized for white dashboards and other pale surfaces.

Do not place the primary white-919 logo on white or pale backgrounds.

### Badge-only applications

Use files in `03-Badge-Mark` for favicons, app icons, avatars, patches, small navigation marks, and square placements where the full name would be unreadable.

The badge represents:

- Fire: burgundy flame
- EMS: white pulse line
- Police: blue star
- Communication: radio and wireless signal
- Unified response: the intertwined red, white, and blue pathways

## Digital and social assets

`04-Digital-Social` contains a square profile image, a 1200 x 630 social-sharing image, and email-ready dark and light lockups.

## Zoom meeting backgrounds

`06-Zoom-Backgrounds` contains three 1920 x 1080 virtual backgrounds with speaker-safe composition:

- Executive Office: polished navy and walnut leadership setting
- Operations Center: public-safety communications environment
- Civic Workspace: bright, welcoming collaboration office

Upload the PNG directly in Zoom under **Settings > Background & effects**. Turn off **Mirror my video** if you want the logo to appear normally in your own self-view; other meeting participants generally see the correct orientation regardless.

## LinkedIn company page

`07-LinkedIn-Page` contains the current 400 x 400 Page logo, a 4200 x 700 branded company-page cover, a clean cover alternative, a compact legacy-size banner, the transparent horizontal lockup, and complete ready-to-paste LinkedIn Page content.

Use the branded cover first. Preview on desktop and mobile before publishing because LinkedIn crops cover images differently across screen sizes.

## Supporting typography

- Headlines: Montserrat ExtraBold or Arial Bold when Montserrat is unavailable
- Body copy and interfaces: Inter or Arial
- The logo wordmark is fixed artwork and must never be retyped or approximated with a font.

## Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space around the full lockup equal to at least half the height of the capital E in EMT. Do not allow navigation, copy, rules, photographs, or other logos inside that area.

- Minimum full-lockup width for digital use: 180 px
- Minimum full-lockup width for print: 1.5 in / 38 mm
- Below those sizes, use the badge-only mark.

## Do not

- Stretch, condense, rotate, skew, or rearrange the artwork.
- Change individual badge colors or remove service symbols.
- Add outlines, glows, drop shadows, or gradients beyond those already in the supplied art.
- Place the logo over a busy image without a solid or sufficiently dark field.
- Use a superseded city-shield, Star-of-Life, or concept-stage EMT919 logo.
- auto-trace, redraw, or separate the raster artwork without brand approval.

See `05-Reference/EMT919_Responder_Badge_Brand_Guide.pdf` for visual examples.

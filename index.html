EMT919 in the field

Dial. Talk. Keep working.

A representative interpretation—not an agency recording. One speaker and one clear turn at a time.

  1. Dial from an authorized phone.

    Use #919 or the dedicated agency line configured for your service.

  2. State the language and the need.

    Speak in short turns. Confirm critical details with the other person.

  3. Escalate when the situation requires it.

    Request a qualified live interpreter according to agency policy and your configured service.

Common questions

Choose with confidence.

Your agency policy, approved configuration and written proposal define the service.

Which product do we need?

Choose EMT919 for field interpretation and language access. Choose 9/Line for officer work-number calls, messages and team communications. A demo can cover either or both.

Does EMT919 replace live interpreters?

No. AI-first mode includes configured live-interpreter escalation, and agencies may choose live-interpreter-only routing. Agency policy determines when a qualified human interpreter is required.

How do we confirm languages and records options?

Bring your language needs and retention policies to the agency review. Availability, escalation, recording and records options are confirmed against the proposed configuration.

Where can our IT and procurement teams start?

Use the trust center, procurement guide and resource center. Request the security package for evidence and agency-specific review.

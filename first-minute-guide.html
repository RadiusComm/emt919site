Field guide

The First Minute.

A practical framework for beginning a multilingual public-safety contact while preserving responder judgment and agency policy.

1. Stabilize the scene

Follow agency safety procedures first. Identify immediate hazards, medical needs, and whether a qualified live interpreter must be used at once.

2. Establish the communication path

From an authorized agency phone, connect to the configured interpretation workflow. Confirm the language when possible and use short, plain statements.

3. Work turn by turn

Speak one idea at a time. Pause for interpretation. Watch for signs that the person did not understand and restate the message when needed.

4. Escalate when the stakes change

Move to a qualified live interpreter when required by law, agency policy, clinical protocol, or the nature of the interaction. Do not rely on an AI-first workflow for a use your agency has not approved.

5. Close and document

Confirm any critical instruction, follow agency documentation rules, and report interpretation or service issues through the designated support path.

This guide is operational planning material, not legal, clinical, or policy advice. Each agency determines approved use.

BEFORE YOU REQUEST

A few useful answers.

What does EMT919 help with?

EMT919 supports turn-by-turn interpretation during in-person conversations through an authorized agency phone. The briefing reviews the configured service path relevant to your team.

Does AI replace qualified interpreters?

No. Agency policy determines when qualified interpretation is required. Review configured live-interpreter escalation and live-only routing against your needs.

Which languages and records options are available?

Confirm language coverage, handoff behavior and available records against your agency’s proposed configuration and contracted services.

What information should I include?

Your agency, work contact details and the questions you want to discuss. Keep case details and sensitive information out of the request.

Can IT and procurement join?

Yes. Include the operational sponsor, prospective deployment administrator and relevant IT, procurement or command staff.

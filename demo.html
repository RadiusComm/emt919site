BEFORE YOU REQUEST

A few useful answers.

Is the overview an actual agency recording?

No. It uses AI-generated promotional imagery. It is not an agency recording or verification of product accuracy, response time or performance. Your agency demo is the opportunity to review the configured workflow.

Does AI replace qualified interpreters?

No. Agency policy determines when qualified interpretation is required. Review configured live-interpreter escalation and live-only routing against your needs.

Which languages and records options are available?

Confirm language coverage, handoff behavior and available records against your agency’s proposed configuration and contracted services.

Who should join the demo?

Include your operational sponsor and prospective deployment administrator. IT, procurement or command staff are welcome. Bring questions and non-sensitive examples, without case details.

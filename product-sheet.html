#919

EMT919 is Emergency Mobile Translation: an agency-configured, telephone-first interpretation workflow for public-safety teams. Authorized users call from a registered phone, communicate turn by turn, and escalate to a qualified live interpreter when required.

How it works

  1. Dial. Start from an authorized agency phone.
  2. Talk. Use short statements and work turn by turn.
  3. Continue or escalate. Follow agency policy and move to live interpretation when required.

Operational fit

  • Law enforcement
  • EMS and fire
  • Corrections
  • Schools and campuses
  • Animal control and code enforcement

Controls

  • TLS 1.2+ in transit
  • AES-256 provider-managed storage encryption
  • Administrative MFA
  • Registered-phone access
  • Agency record isolation and audit logging
  • No training on customer audio or transcripts

Commercial overview

$299 per officer / year

Final minimums, configuration, live-interpreter terms, onboarding, support, and renewal details are stated in the written quote.

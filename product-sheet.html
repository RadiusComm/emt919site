#919

EMT919 is Emergency MultiTranslator: an agency-configured, telephone-first interpretation workflow for public-safety teams. Authorized users call from a registered phone, communicate turn by turn, and escalate to a qualified live interpreter when required.

How it works

  1. Dial. Start from an authorized agency phone.
  2. Talk. Use short statements and work turn by turn.
  3. Continue or escalate. Follow agency policy and move to live interpretation when required.

Operational fit

  • Law enforcement
  • EMS and fire
  • Corrections
  • Schools and campuses
  • Animal control and code enforcement

Controls

  • TLS 1.2+ in transit
  • AES-256 provider-managed storage encryption
  • Administrative MFA
  • Registered-phone access
  • Agency record isolation and audit logging
  • No training on customer audio or transcripts

Service options

  • AI-first interpretation
  • EMT-managed live escalation
  • Routing to an existing interpreter provider
  • Optional Evidentiary Package

Commercial terms and final scope are stated in the written agency proposal.

Important boundary: EMT919 supports communication; it does not replace agency policy, legal or clinical judgment, or qualified live interpretation when required. FastDial Inc, operator of EMT919 does not currently hold its own SOC 2 report; provider certifications apply to the named providers.

Retention boundary

Voice audio follows a 30-day default. Transcript retention is defined in the agency agreement, and longer transcript retention requires the additional storage option. Contract terms and legal or agency preservation holds control when they differ from ordinary deletion.

Data location and use

Customer platform data is stored and processed in the United States. Customer audio, transcripts, and call narratives are not used to train Fast Dial or subprocessor AI models.

Change review

Current subprocessor information and supporting provider materials are supplied to authorized reviewers. Contract-specific notice and objection terms, when applicable, are defined in the agency agreement.

EMT919 is a service of Fast Dial, Inc. d/b/a City919.

What we do

City919 builds telephone-first communication services for time-sensitive public-sector interactions. EMT919 is Emergency Mobile Translation: an agency-configured interpretation workflow designed to begin from a phone officers and responders already carry.

Security review

Direct support

Public-safety communication, built to be accountable.

EMT919 is a service of Fast Dial, Inc. d/b/a City919.

What we do

City919 builds telephone-first communication services for time-sensitive public-sector interactions. EMT919 is Emergency MultiTranslator: an agency-configured interpretation workflow designed to begin from a phone officers and responders already carry.

How we operate

Claims discipline

We distinguish product capabilities, provider certifications, internal controls, and verified customer outcomes.

Human escalation

AI-first service includes live interpreter escalation; agencies may also select live-interpreter-only routing.

Security review

Architecture, subprocessors, retention, access controls, and incident response are documented for agency review.

Direct support

Agency contacts can reach a named operational owner for onboarding, security review, and incident coordination.

Company identity

Corporate, insurance, tax, and contracting documentation is provided directly to authorized public-sector buyers during procurement review.

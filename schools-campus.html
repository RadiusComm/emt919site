Schools and campus safety

Communicate with families in the moment.

Support initial communication among public-safety staff, students, parents, visitors, and campus personnel while preserving school and agency policy.

Representative scenario

A school resource officer needs to explain where a parent should wait and confirm who is authorized to pick up a student. The officer uses an authorized phone to start a turn-by-turn interpreted conversation.

Appropriate boundaries

EMT919 is not a substitute for mandated disability accommodations, special-education procedures, parental-notice requirements, legal advice, or qualified interpretation where policy or law requires it. Do not include protected student information beyond what the approved workflow requires.

Typical questions

  • Who are you here to meet?
  • What language do you prefer?
  • Is anyone injured or in immediate danger?
  • Please wait in this location while staff verify the next step.

Responsible AI

Useful within clear limits.

EMT919 supports communication. It does not replace professional judgment, agency policy, legal advice, clinical judgment, or a qualified interpreter when one is required.

Operating boundaries

Human remains accountable

The responder remains responsible for the interaction and follows agency policy. EMT919 does not make enforcement, medical, legal, eligibility, or disciplinary decisions.

Escalation is part of the workflow

AI-first service includes access to live interpreter escalation. Agencies may select a live-interpreter-only configuration.

High-stakes communication

Miranda warnings, consent, legal notices, clinical decisions, disciplinary matters, and other policy-controlled uses require agency-approved procedures and a qualified interpreter when required.

No training on agency content

Customer audio, transcripts, and call narratives are not used to train Fast Dial or subprocessor AI models.

Known limitations

Interpretation quality can be affected by noise, overlapping speech, poor cellular service, uncommon terminology, accents, device placement, and unclear phrasing. EMT919 does not publish a universal connection-time or latency guarantee because field conditions and third-party networks vary.

Agency evaluation

Agencies should test representative languages, environments, escalation paths, and failure conditions before operational approval. The agency evaluation standard provides a repeatable starting point.

Report a concern

Security concerns, harmful output, translation quality issues, or unexpected system behavior can be reported to security@city919.com.

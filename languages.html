When a live interpreter joins

Configured handoff: When in-call handoff is enabled, the responder can request a live interpreter on the same service path without beginning a second agency workflow. Exact behavior depends on the agency configuration and contracted provider.

Responder request

The responder can request a live interpreter when the configured service includes live escalation.

Language outside AI coverage

The service can route to the agency's selected live-interpreter option when the requested language is not supported by AI-first mode.

Policy-controlled interaction

Agency policy may require a qualified live interpreter for legal, medical, disciplinary, or other high-stakes communication.

Audio or interpretation difficulty

Noise, overlapping speech, uncommon terminology, or unclear output should trigger the agency's approved escalation or alternate path.

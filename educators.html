Questions from educators

A clear next step
starts with answers.

We’ll help your team review the proposed service, language coverage, and school requirements.

Do teachers or families need to download an app?

No additional app or dedicated hardware is required for EMT919’s phone-based interpretation workflow. Your school’s demonstration will cover authorized phones, access, and the setup for your intended conversations.

Which languages are available?

EMT919 provides AI interpretation in its supported languages. Availability and live-interpreter options depend on the proposed service. Bring your school’s language needs to the demonstration so we can confirm coverage. Review language options.

Is this for classroom teachers or administrators?

Both. Teachers can explore everyday learning and family conversations. Front-office staff, school leaders, and district teams can evaluate enrollment, outreach, attendance, and other approved school workflows.

Can our school or district evaluate it first?

Request a school demonstration and tell us what you want to try. We can discuss evaluation or pilot options, the participating staff, language needs, and purchasing requirements. Scope, pricing, and service terms are confirmed with your organization.

What about student privacy and sensitive conversations?

Review data handling, access, recording, and retention with your district’s IT and privacy teams before use. Keep demonstrations free of student records and identifying details. AI interpretation can make mistakes; follow school policy and use qualified interpreters for sensitive or high-stakes discussions, including special-education and disciplinary matters, whenever required.

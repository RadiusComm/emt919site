How the word score is calculated

Substituted, missing and extra words reduce each distance’s match to the 16-word script. Each complete trial maps 0–100% word match to 1–10; the three distances contribute equally. Conflicting distance wording requires a repeat, not a low device rating. A failed analysis has no score. Recognition can be wrong, particularly with noise; this is not certified recording or call quality.

      Results and privacy limits

      Only the measured 5-, 10- and 20-foot distances are tested. No result is inferred for other distances. Results are digital audio-level measurements, not verified native 9/Line compatibility scores, and apply only to the distances and conditions tested. Browser capture can differ from 9/Line’s native audio pipeline. This tests the microphone; speaker and earpiece tests are separate.

      Recordings stay in memory and disappear on reload or close. No audio uploads, recording downloads, persistent recording history or analytics. Speech recognition runs locally after you start. About 66 MB of model/runtime files may download and be cached; recognized text stays temporary on this device and is never uploaded. Public submission is optional and requires explicit consent. Only newly consented model and numeric results appear in the roster. Hosting infrastructure processes ordinary connection information.

      Microphone access needs HTTPS and browser support. Open this page directly if an embedded preview blocks it.

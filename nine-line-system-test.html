01

Set the scene

Open this page directly on the phone you’re testing. Keep its position and your speaking volume consistent between recordings.

Select the actual device; nothing is inferred from your browser. This is a starter list, not an exhaustive catalog. Apple model labels · Samsung model labels · Google model label

The OS defaults from the selected device family; you can change it. Other models allow a manual OS choice. Enter the OS version yourself; changing device or OS clears the previous version.

Changes to the phone, room or position mean a new setup. Clear this session before comparing a new setup.

02

Record & listen

First, capture about 5 seconds of room noise without speaking. This is a background reference, not a clarity score.

Use a tape measure. Any whole-foot distance from 1 to 30 is available. Audio level cannot measure distance.

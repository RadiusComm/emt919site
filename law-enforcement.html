Law enforcement

Keep the contact moving.

Telephone-first interpretation for the opening minutes of traffic stops, victim contacts, domestic calls, crisis response, witness interviews, welfare checks, and community interactions.

Designed around the officer

The officer initiates the call from an authorized phone, states what must be communicated, and continues turn by turn. No public download or new belt-worn device is required.

Additional field workflows

Witness interviews

Begin gathering an account while preserving speaker roles and moving to qualified live interpretation when policy requires it.

Welfare checks

Ask immediate safety questions, explain why responders are present, and clarify next steps.

Dispatch support

Use the agency-configured language path before or during responder contact. Availability depends on the department's approved workflow.

Victim contact

Begin essential communication while maintaining the agency's rules for legal notices, consent, records, and human interpretation.

Policy stays in control

EMT919 supports communication; it does not replace agency policy, legal review, or qualified live interpretation where required. Live escalation is available, and agencies may select live-interpreter-only routing.

Law enforcement

Keep the contact moving.

Telephone-first interpretation for the opening minutes of traffic stops, victim contacts, domestic calls, crisis response, schools, and community interactions.

Designed around the officer

The officer initiates the call from an authorized phone, states what must be communicated, and continues turn by turn. No public download or new belt-worn device is required.

Policy stays in control

EMT919 supports communication; it does not replace agency policy, legal review, or qualified live interpretation where required. Live escalation is available, and agencies may select live-interpreter-only routing.

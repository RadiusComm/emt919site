Case-study standard

Future case studies will be published only after written agency authorization. Each study must identify the agency and authorized spokesperson, define the operational setting and measured period, explain the source of every quantitative result, disclose relevant limitations, and receive final agency approval before publication.

Until those approvals exist, EMT919 will not substitute anonymous quotes, invented badges, or implied customer adoption.

Publication cadence

The resource program is structured around one useful operational article or brief each month, one security or procurement update each quarter, and case studies whenever a participating agency authorizes publication.
